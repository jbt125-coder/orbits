Add an object

Add in orbit gives it its real orbit shape and distance, including how oval it is (eccentricity). The Moon goes around Earth and planets go around the Sun. In the solar system scenes, planets appear where they really are on the date shown. Place by hand lets you touch a spot and drag to aim it yourself.

More

Masses, gravity and orbit tilts are real, in full 3D. Distances and sizes are squeezed so the Moon and Neptune fit on one screen: each orbit is drawn around the body it circles, at a compressed distance. Drag two fingers up or down, or use the Tilt slider, to look from the side. Height lines drop from each object to the flat plane of Earth's orbit, so you can see what rides above or below it.
